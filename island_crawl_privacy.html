Privacy Policy

Island Crawl · Doophie
Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Island Crawl (the “Game”), developed by Doophie (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information. The Game is primarily offline, but uses certain Google services for advertising, analytics, diagnostics, purchases, leaderboards, and cloud saves.

1. Information We Collect

Island Crawl is primarily an offline, single-player game. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or other personal information to access core gameplay. Google services integrated into the Game may automatically process device and app identifiers, device and operating system information, gameplay events, diagnostics, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, purchase status, Google Play Games identifiers, leaderboard scores, and saved game data. The information processed depends on your device, settings, and enabled features.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

Island Crawl uses Google AdMob to show advertisements, including optional rewarded ads. AdMob may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where applicable, personalize ads. The Game currently does not provide an in-app advertising consent management screen. This description does not imply that any consent required by law has been obtained. You can review advertising preferences in your Google account and device settings. See Google's privacy and advertising policies linked below.

3. Analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) to understand app usage and improve the Game. Depending on configuration, it may process app instance identifiers, gameplay events, session activity, device information, app version, and approximate geographic information.

4. Crash Reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and improve stability. Crashlytics may process crash logs, diagnostic information, device and operating system details, app version, and installation identifiers.

5. In-App Purchases — Google Play Billing

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not directly receive your payment card details. The Game may process purchased product identifiers, transaction status, and purchase tokens to verify and deliver purchases. Google's payment processing is subject to its privacy practices.

6. Google Play Games — Leaderboards and Cloud Saves

Google Play Games Services provides leaderboards and cloud saves. Depending on the features you use, Google may process your Play Games player identifier, display name, leaderboard scores, rankings, and saved gameplay data. Leaderboard information may be visible to other players. Cloud saving and restoration depend on your account and service availability.

7. Local Game Data

The Game stores settings, progress, and unlocked content on your device. Some progress may also be synchronized using Google Play Games cloud saves. Uninstalling the Game, clearing app data, or changing devices can result in loss of data that has not been backed up.

8. Third-Party Services and Sharing

We use Google AdMob (advertising), Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics), Firebase Crashlytics (diagnostics), Google Play Billing (purchases), and Google Play Games Services (leaderboards and saves). These services process information as described in their own privacy disclosures. Doophie does not independently sell player information to data brokers. Advertising-related processing by Google is described in Google's policies.

9. Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. Third-party providers maintain their own security and retention practices. Information may be processed in countries other than your own. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restrictions on processing of personal information. You can manage some Google advertising and Play Games preferences through your Google account and Android settings. For requests concerning information controlled by Doophie, contact us below; requests concerning Google's independently controlled information may need to be directed to Google.

11. Children's Privacy

Island Crawl is generally intended for teenage and adult audiences; its content rating varies by region. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Third-party services may process information as described above. The app's declared target audience and advertising configuration must comply with applicable children's privacy requirements. Parents or guardians may contact us with concerns.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when the Game, services, or legal requirements change. We will update the date at the top of this page and provide additional notice where required.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Doophie at quinn@doophie.ca.
