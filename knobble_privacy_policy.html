Privacy Policy

Knobble · Doophie
Last updated: November 10, 2026

Knobble (the “App”) is a rotary Android launcher developed by Doophie (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Knobble is designed to work without tracking users or collecting personal information for Doophie. This policy explains how the App uses local information and how Android backup features may handle app settings.

1. Information Collection

Knobble does not collect personal information for Doophie. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or activity tracking. No account is required to use the launcher.

2. Installed Applications

To function as an Android launcher, Knobble accesses information about applications available on your device so it can display and launch them through its rotary interface. This information is processed on your device and is not sent to Doophie or its servers.

3. Local Storage and Android Backup

Knobble stores launcher preferences and settings on your device. Android's built-in backup and restore features may back up eligible app data to your Google account or another backup provider, depending on your device, Android version, and backup settings. Backups are managed by Android and the backup provider, not Doophie. Doophie does not receive or access these backups. You can manage backup options in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Platform Services

Knobble does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. If you install Knobble through Google Play, Google may independently process information related to installation, updates, and store activity. Android and your selected backup provider may also process backed-up app data under their own privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing and Security

Doophie does not sell or share personal information collected through Knobble because Knobble does not collect it. App settings are stored locally and may be included in Android-managed backups. Device and backup security are governed by Android and the applicable backup provider.

6. Children's Privacy

Knobble does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Its launcher functionality does not require users to submit personal information.

7. Your Choices

You can clear Knobble's locally stored data through Android settings, uninstall the App, and manage Android backup settings through your device or backup provider. Removing local app data may not automatically delete copies retained in a device backup; consult your backup provider's controls.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Knobble's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published with a revised date. If future versions introduce collection or transmission of information, we will update this disclosure accordingly.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Doophie at quinn@doophie.ca.
